# RANalyzer v0.9 — customer journey acceptance

## Question 1: What is the offering?
**Present in copy and interface:** A source-linked engineering investigation brief (measured symptoms, qualified possible mechanisms, evidence gaps, next tests). Buyer roles include RAN Engineering, Network Assurance and Managed Services. The product is not claimed to be a production RCA engine or site-acceptance certifier.

## Question 2: What do the scenarios establish?
- RAN-01: high loss and severely impaired receiver throughput, with sampled protocol concerns but no verified cause.
- RAN-02: sender/receiver disparity must not be confused with receiver-reported packet loss.
- RAN-03: seven zero-reception intervals, radio indicators, and missing flow/time alignment.

Each Case Stories scene connects its evidence reveal to why that stage matters, and the final scene presents the deliverable.

## Question 3: What is the purpose of the Agent Flow?
A separate technical transparency experience showing the saved SP03, SP04 and Supervisor records with trace timing and a downstream semantic Judge. It is not live execution or model thought-process access.
